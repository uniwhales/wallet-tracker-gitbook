# AI Copilot

AI Copilot is a chat assistant inside the Cielo terminal. Ask it about any token, wallet or your own trading, and it answers from the same wallet data you trade with. It's in beta.

{% embed url="https://x.com/CieloFinance/status/2122735756489679280" %}

## Open it

Click **AI Copilot** at the bottom of any page. It opens as a panel, so you can keep the chart and order panel in view while you ask.

The chip at the top shows what Copilot is answering about, usually the page you're on, for example **Asking about Bonk on Solana**. Click it to keep a token in focus, or to ask without a page.

## What to ask

* Who the top traders of a token are, and what they're buying now
* Whether a wallet is worth copying before you copy it
* How your own trading is going

You can also track wallets and set alerts straight from the chat.

Type `/` for shortcuts: token check, wallet review, copy setup and your PnL. Under each answer, Copilot suggests follow-up questions you can tap.

## Scheduled reports

Like an answer? Say "every morning" and Copilot drafts a scheduled report, delivered in the app or to your Telegram. Your reports live in the **Scheduled** tab.

## Memory

Say "remember..." and Copilot keeps it for next time, like the chains you trade or the wallets you care about. You can see what it remembers in the **Memory** tab.

## Usage

The meter at the top of the panel shows how much of your Copilot usage you have left. **History** opens past chats, and **New chat** starts a fresh one.
