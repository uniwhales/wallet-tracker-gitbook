# Referrals

Share your referral link and you earn a share of the trading fees paid by everyone who signs up with it. That includes their manual trades and the trades their copy trading agents make. Your share goes up as you move up the referral ranks.

Open the [Referrals](https://app.cielo.finance/referrals) page from the account menu in the top right.

## Your referral link

Your link looks like `app.cielo.finance/r/YOURCODE`. Click **Edit** to change the code to something easier to remember.

You can also add your code to any profile or token link, so people land on the page you're sharing and still count as your referral:

* `app.cielo.finance/profile/YOURCODE_<wallet address>`
* `app.cielo.finance/trading/solana/YOURCODE_<token address>`

{% hint style="info" %}
If you add a Cielo bot to a Telegram group or public channel, the buy buttons in its alerts carry your referral code. Anyone who signs up by tapping them becomes your referral.
{% endhint %}

## Ranks

Everyone starts at Rank 1, and the Referrals page shows your current rank.

| Rank | Your share of referred fees |
| ---- | --------------------------- |
| 1    | 10%                         |
| 2    | 15%                         |
| 3    | 20%                         |
| 4    | 25%                         |
| 5    | 30%                         |

## Claiming

Earnings are kept per chain: Solana, Base, Robinhood and Arc. Each chain shows its total earned, and **Claim and Share** shows what's available to claim on the chain you've selected. Earnings aren't paid out automatically: click **Claim All** on the Referrals page to collect them.

At the bottom of the page you'll see everyone who joined with your link, when they joined and how much you've earned from each.

{% hint style="info" %}
Cashback and trading points have been retired. Referral fees are the only rewards program.
{% endhint %}
