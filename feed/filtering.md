# Filters

By default, your feed displays the latest tx from all the wallets you’re following. Use the side menu to filter results. These allow you to focus on a specific list, use case, network, tx type, and size.

**Network:** Filter to display tx from specific networks. You can make multiple selections.

**Signal Types**: Filter to display specific tx e.g _Swaps_. You can make multiple selections.

**Token Search:** Enter a token or NFT name e.g. _ARB_ or select from the Top Tokens popup. You can make multiple selections.

**Amount USD**: Enter a minimum tx value between 1 and 250,000 (USD).

**Lists**: Filter to show wallet activity from specific lists you're following.

**Only show new trades**: Show only a wallet's first trade in each token.

**Multi Buys**: Show only tokens several of your wallets bought in a short window.

**Market Cap**, **Token Age** and **Launchpads**: Show only swaps of tokens inside a market cap range, an age range, or from certain launchpads.

Using these options, you can set multiple filters that will determine the tx shown in your feed. After enabling filters, click **Save** and enter a label to store these settings for future use e.g. _New Swaps on Base >$10K_.

Any time you wish to reset the filters you’ve applied, click **Clear all**.

To load saved filters, open the presets dropdown at the top of the sidebar.

## Summary

Your Cielo feed is a powerful tool for viewing onchain transactions and making sense of them at a glance. These could be your own trades, those of influencers you’re following, or of an entire sector you’re trying to monitor e.g. DeFi.
