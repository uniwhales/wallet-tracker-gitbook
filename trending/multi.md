# Multi Buys

One wallet buying a token tells you about that wallet. Several of your wallets buying the same token within minutes of each other tells you something about the token. Multi Buys pulls those moments out of your Feed so you can see confluence at a glance, including the ones that happened while you were away.

Turn on **Multi Buys** in the Feed filters. It works in both Classic and Lite, on every chain you track.

## Settings

| Setting        | What it controls                                                              |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wallets**    | How many of your tracked wallets must buy the token.                          |
| **Timeframe**  | How close together the buys must be. Shorter windows mean tighter confluence. |
| **Market cap** | Skip tokens outside this range.                                               |
| **Min USD**    | Ignore buys smaller than this, so dust buys don't count.                      |

## Act on it

* **Alerts**: create a [Multiple Wallet alert](../wallet-tracking/custom-alerts/multiple-wallet.md) (Pro and Whale) to get the same signal in Telegram or Discord.
* **Automate it**: a [Multi-Buy trading agent](../trading-agents/overview.md#signal-types) buys automatically when the signal fires, with your size, filters and exits.
