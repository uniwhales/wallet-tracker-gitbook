# Getting Started

<figure><img src="https://1250667054-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FgZTvWhkjLJ8uCLtiUaPq%2Fuploads%2Fbj6ApjFIAa2urWgIGUh3%2FScreenshot%202026-01-13%20at%2014.47.08.png?alt=media&#x26;token=c1d83056-0ee6-4088-9496-6e3bfa1f6fa6" alt=""><figcaption><p>Default view of the Cielo web app.</p></figcaption></figure>

Cielo is packed with powerful features including advanced tools for professional wallet trackers and traders. You don't need to be familiar with all of these to start benefiting from Cielo though. In fact, you can be up and running in as little as 60 seconds.

<figure><img src="https://1250667054-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FgZTvWhkjLJ8uCLtiUaPq%2Fuploads%2FY5k6eHs4CddZHlGlHOfk%2FScreenshot%202026-01-13%20at%2016.23.57.png?alt=media&#x26;token=b85b5107-c5b1-4005-9df7-b6649d2d0f7a" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* Visit the Cielo [web app](https://app.cielo.finance/) and click the **Connect** button in the top right. The above popup will appear and you can connect your web wallet (including Robinhood Wallet) or sign in with Google, X, or Telegram. (Click the Solana tab to select an EVM wallet if you prefer.)
* If you connected a wallet, click to sign the message that pops up. This is used to verify ownership of the wallet you're connecting with and does _not_ authorize Cielo to access any funds.

With these two steps completed, you're now connected to the Cielo web app and able to start your voyage of onchain discovery, from tracking wallets to trading Solana tokens.

If you're interested in Solana trading, the final step before you can get started is to deposit some SOL to the trading wallet that Cielo auto-generates when you first connect to the web app.

<figure><img src="https://1250667054-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FgZTvWhkjLJ8uCLtiUaPq%2Fuploads%2Fi5LUFhstDLusakTzAckn%2FScreenshot%202026-01-14%20at%2008.20.12.png?alt=media&#x26;token=56af26f2-8655-4b9c-9f12-b8adfc6afc01" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In the top right of the page, click the red wallet icon to view the address of your Solana trading wallet. Click **Deposit** and click again to agree to the terms and conditions. You can now send SOL to your trading wallet and get started.

If you're not looking to start trading at this time, and would rather use Cielo for wallet tracking you can skip this step. Instead, follow the guide below to begin tracking your first wallets and receiving Telegram alerts:

1. Go to [Tracking](https://app.cielo.finance/tracking), click **Track Wallets** (top right), enter a wallet address and add a label. Repeat.
2. Alternatively, open the **Public Lists** tab in [Tracking](https://app.cielo.finance/tracking), find an interesting list and click **Follow**.
3. In **Tracking**, create your own list by clicking **Create a List**.
4. Tick the wallets you wish to add and choose your list from the bar at the bottom of the page.
5. To set Telegram/Discord alerts, first go to **Settings >** [**Alerts**](https://app.cielo.finance/settings/alerts) and activate a bot.
6. Then in **Tracking** tick the box to the left of the wallets you wish to set alerts for or click **Select All**.
7. Next, click **Alert Settings** (bottom of page) or click the edit icon to the right of the wallet label.
8. Finally pick the transaction types, USD value, and networks you wish to receive Telegram/Discord alerts for.
9. View recent transactions made by wallets you’re following in [**Feed**](https://app.cielo.finance/feed). Use the options in the right sidebar to filter results.
10. Unlock additional features including the ability to track more wallets in **Settings >** [**Plans**](https://app.cielo.finance/settings/plans).

There's a lot more to Cielo, including an array of advanced trading and tracking features, but in this Quick Start Guide we've covered the basics. Read on to learn more about how Cielo works, starting with **Trending** – your gateway to Solana token discovery.
