# Subscribe

The Cielo web app and Telegram/Discord bots are free to use. Up to 250 EVM wallets and 50 Solana wallets can be tracked, as well as 5 tokens and 5 lists, with no subscription. To access additional features, including tracking for thousands of wallets, Pro and Whale tier plans are available.

**Pro** users can access four Telegram bots (including 2 private) and track 1,000 EVM and 200 Solana wallets, while **Whale** users can access 18 Telegram bots (including 9 private) and track 10,000 EVM and 1,000 Solana wallets.<br>

### Pro

* Enhanced web app
* Follow 1,000 EVM wallets and 200 Solana wallets
* Track 10 tokens
* Create 10 lists
* 2 Discord channels
* 4 Telegram bots (2 private)
* 1,000 alerts per hour
* Hyperliquid alerts
* Exclusions
* Wallet discovery<br>

### Whale

* Enhanced web app
* Follow 10,000 EVM wallets
* Follow 1,000 Solana wallets
* Track 30 tokens
* Create 50 lists
* 20 Discord channels
* 18 Telegram bots (9 private)
* 3,000 alerts per hour
* Hyperliquid alerts
* Exclusions
* Wallet discovery
* Onchain summary
* API

Pro costs $59 per month ($49 per month billed annually). Whale costs $199 per month ($166 per month billed annually).

Learn more about Cielo plans and upgrade [**here**](https://app.cielo.finance/settings/plans). Pro and Whale plans can be purchased for one month, six months, or 12 months using credit/debit card. They can alternatively be purchased for six months or 12 months using crypto. Should your subscription expire, your Cielo account will revert to free and your permissions will reduce accordingly.
